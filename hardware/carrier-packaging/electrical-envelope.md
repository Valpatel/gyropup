# Electrical inventory and packaging constraints — work in progress

This document owns the electrical side of [issue96](https://github.com/Valpatel/gyropup/issues/96). It does not change the native circuit or fabrication geometry. The source contract remains 15–20.5 V stand /30–42 V robot input, 12 V PAN output and a 3.25 A screening load; charged-front-output and transient bounds remain unqualified.

The initial reproducible inventory contains **47 electronic items**: 28 retained cutoff/converter candidate parts after replacing its series diode, all ten items in the LTC4359 Figure10 reference network, and nine legacy control-board parts after replacing the old converter. It is deliberately **not** a complete physical BOM yet: harness protection, actual junctions/terminations, connectors/contacts, retention and the heat path must also be assigned. [Inventory](component-inventory.json), generated by `python hardware/carrier-packaging/inventory.py`, binds the current reviewed circuit, legacy component source and catalog. P.*, I.* and L.* keep the three designator namespaces separate.

## Physical negotiation

Prefer retaining the entire PAN electronics with the removable carrier. The existing control board remains occupied; no schematic placeholder or old U2 reservation supplies extra area. An initial 18 mm assembled-height budget is a design question, not a measured envelope. No contour or physical stack is frozen.

The mechanical author's source inspection found that the current parked C3 body **and its mandatory 19.1 mm straight wire exit** obstruct a tall +Y addition during +X withdrawal. Lowering the entire board below that reservation is not sufficient: the fixed pan-base drip skirt occupies Z0..18 and is also crossed during withdrawal. These are concrete constraints on this service path, not proof that every possible layout fails.

A single alternative is being negotiated: a 90° turn of the fixed C3 housing at the outboard service position before inward parking, paired with a trial electronics region X4..48, Y53..93, Z23..41 mm. This **44×40×18 mm overall region is not allocated or validated space**. It extends beyond the current bumper; full cable, retention, seated/removal/parked geometry and thermal constraints remain to be checked. Components must be placed with real lands, per-side heights, mounting and terminal zones before accepting any board dimensions. The manufacturer's 40×40 mm thermal example is not the proposed outline's justification.

## Verified package evidence collected so far

[Primary source record](primary-envelope-sources.json) preserves observations and limits. Exact part bodies, manufacturer solder recommendations, optional library courtyards and additional design clearances must remain distinct.

- Panasonic EEUFC2A220: exact 8×11.5 mm nominal case; FC drawing tolerances give **8.5 mm maximum diameter and 12.5 mm maximum body length**, before lead/mounting and vent clearances. This exceeds a casual nominal-height-only model.
- Pololu5577: the manufacturer drawing distinguishes **6.1 mm above the PCB, 1.57 mm PCB thickness, and 1.4 mm below it** for items5570–5577. All three regions matter; profile tolerances are not supplied. Twelve electrical holes and three mounting holes require separate orientation/numbering and access checks. A zero-bottom-height box is invalid.
- TNPW0805: maximum body **2.1×1.4×0.55 mm** from the current manufacturer drawing; land recommendations are a separate input.
- WSL2512 10/20 mΩ: maximum body **6.604×3.434×0.889 mm**; the manufacturer's applicable land row gives **7.36×3.68 mm** total span. Shared Kelvin endpoints and copper routing remain additional constraints.

## Open source and interface gates

The actual LTC4359 Rev F PDF and Figure10 pixels have now been inspected independently by three agents. [Reference contract](front-reference-contract.json) binds the document and rendered schematic/pin-page hashes, exact physical U1/Q1 pin identities and every diode polarity. No new native circuit is generated. The floating VSS is separate from system GND. The 47 nF capacitor returns to floating VSS; the separately drawn CLOAD returns to system GND. The retained P.C1 sits **after the cutoff pair**, so it cannot be treated as permanently connected front-output capacitance. The front load's effective capacitance/leakage by cutoff state remains a circuit-validation obligation.

The DCB6 exposed pad is electrically NC in this paper proposal, following page11's guidance to increase effective spacing above30 V. Pin6 still returns to floating VSS; the physical exposed metal is not omitted from the land envelope. Actual creepage, contamination/leakage and thermal behavior remain unresolved. The SHDN pin remains open as drawn in Figure10, with its leakage condition visible. This is not approval to route a compact high-voltage board merely because the IC fits.

[Package records](package-envelopes.json) currently provide primary evidence for27 of47 electronic items; they distinguish maximum body dimensions, nominal module profiles and manufacturer lands. They do not establish a complete footprint/terminal inventory or a placement. The Panasonic valve requires at least2 mm of free space: its upright maximum body plus valve allowance alone is14.5 mm, before mounting, PCB, underside solder and insulation. The18 mm trial stack therefore cannot be accepted from nominal component heights.

The mechanical investigation now also holds the region on the bumper teardown sequence. The existing C3 support obstructs a pure upward rigid bumper lift, while some carrier/support fasteners require the bumper removed for the currently modelled straight tools. The proposed electronics overhang adds an obstruction. No new board placement is frozen while a viable rigid teardown path or explicit retention redesign is missing.

The conditional CJT A2505 servo connector from the previous carrier note has a **3 A family rating**, so it does not yet satisfy the current 3.25 A screening contract. Its OEM conductor/cavity mapping is also unverified. Do not allocate its envelope as a qualified final servo termination. Shared C2/C3 BUS remains raw BUS_HEAD_FUSED; protected PAN_INPUT must never become the slip-ring bus. CAN and TTL bypass routes still require actual endpoints and insulated retention; no unassigned pad strip can stand in for them.

Next: complete maximum body/land/lead and terminal inventories, resolve the mechanical teardown gate, then create one explicit nonoverlapping component/terminal assignment and per-side stack in agreement with the mechanical envelope. No new native circuit/CAD, purchase, powered test or fit/thermal qualification is produced by this inventory checkpoint.

## Reproduce the current bounded checks

Run `python hardware/carrier-packaging/inventory.py`, then `python hardware/carrier-packaging/check.py` and `python -m unittest discover -s hardware/carrier-packaging -p 'test_*.py'`. Eight tests include dropped/duplicate items, stale source or dimensions, improper readiness promotion, reversed clamps, grounded floating reference/capacitor and mistaken direct load-capacitor assignment. These are inventory/reference controls, not EDA/ERC, physical collision, thermal or waveform validation.
