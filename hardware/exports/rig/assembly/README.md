# Archived leg-rig assembly illustrations

The images and `steps.json` in this directory are historical assembly evidence,
not a current print, fastening or operating instruction. They have not been
regenerated for the corrected RS-04 mounting candidate.

In particular, step 7's recorded **10 mm counterbore and 2.5 N·m motor-lug torque
are withdrawn**. Its claims about proven coupon fits are not physical test
results. The original JSON is retained to preserve the historical record.

Use the [current mounting-fit evidence](../../../docs/rs04-mounting-fit.md) and
[unpowered observation job](../../../docs/rs04-fit-job.md). Eight support checks
still fail; mounting preload, thread strength, actual fit and full-leg operation
remain unqualified. The [assembly page](../../../docs/leg-rig-assembly.md) carries
this status, and its corrected generator preserves it on future regeneration.
