01 / THE BIG IDEA

Big robots.
Backyard legends.

Meet your next
favorite rival.

A self-balancing robot. A foam-ball turret. A personality you can’t help rooting for. Gyropup is the beginning of a robot family that rolls, dodges, and battles alongside you.

Our big idea: 50-pound-class robot playmates for foam-ball battles, target challenges, and backyard missions. Explore the simulated prototype today while we work toward the real thing.

Meet Gyropup in the simulator ↗

The ambition is 50-pound-class play. The current CAD design is 37.5 kg (83 lb), and weight reduction is part of the engineering ahead.

02 / AN EXPERIMENT YOU CAN EXPLORE

Built by
autonomous systems.

Robots made with the help of AI, from software to hardware. Gyropup is a Valpatel Software demo of autonomous engineering: agents write code, develop CAD, run simulations, and document their progress—with human direction and review.
