ALWAYS IMPROVING · WITH CAD-AGENT

Checks that make
two projects better.

Gyropup is designed with cad-agent, an open-source, general-purpose CAD toolkit for agents. Its checks find problems for Gyropup to fix, and each new kind of check becomes a tested cad-agent tool, ready for any project.

MOTION TRACES · CAD-AGENT #61

A Gyropup bug became a tool

0.00001 mmworst error, 48 parts × 46 poses (limit 0.02 mm)

Turret parts in the browser once swung about the wrong pivot, 96 mm off at 180°. Gyropup fixed its axes (#42); cad-agent gained a motion-trace check for every project.

JOINT AUDIT · CAD-AGENT #230

Every insert and clamped joint

In progress

cad-agent is adding checks for heat-set inserts and compression limiters: hole and boss size, pull-out, torque-out and preload. Gyropup’s joints are the worked example.

Follow the work ↗
