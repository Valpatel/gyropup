Simulation results

Every design runs the same scenarios in MuJoCo with a balance controller derived from its own model. Motor limits come from the catalog at the design's battery voltage. Simulation only: nothing here has been tested on hardware. Run tools/simulate.py --all --video to refresh.

Overnight design loop

The optimiser (tools/iterate.py) builds and checks candidates end to end: designer checks, CAD, clashes, the physics scenarios, stiffness and strength, cables. Every part is printed; it trades mass, print hours, motor load and width.
