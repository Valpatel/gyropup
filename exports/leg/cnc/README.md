## output_adapter (x2 per leg, x4 per robot)

- File: `output_adapter.step` (Aluminium 6061-T6 (machined), 152.5 g, 63.5 x 63.5 x 27.6 mm)
- Order: JLCCNC (jlccnc.com: upload the STEP for the instant price); backup Xometry (xometry.com: instant quote from the STEP)
- Price: an estimate until the instant price is read on upload (D40: no quotes; JLCCNC turned-and-milled 6061, qty 4); shipping from China is extra and takes 5-10 days (estimate $40 each)

**Callouts**

- Material 6061-T6; as machined, deburred; no anodise (it would change the journal); general tolerance ISO 2768-m; threads 6H
- Datums: A = the Ø60 journal's axis; the contact face (z 0) square to A within 0.02; the standard face (z 24.6) parallel to the contact face within 0.02; the dowel holes and the motor's pin sockets at position Ø0.05 to A
- Ø60 j6 journal, z 11.6 to 24.6, with a 0.5 x 45 deg lead-in at the face end: the support bearing's inner ring (61812), fitted with retaining compound
- Ø63.5 shoulder, its face at z 11.6 square to the axis within 0.02: seats the bearing's inner ring
- Contact face at z 0 (Ø48.8) flat within 0.02; 0.6 mm relief outside it
- 8 x M5 tapped 16 deep (drill Ø4.2 x 19) from the face (z 24.6), PCD 50 at 0, 30, 90, 120, 180, 210, 270, 300 deg
- 2 x Ø5 H7 x 8 deep from the face, PCD 50 at 60, 150 deg (Ø5 m6 dowel pins pressed in)
- 9 x Ø5.5 through, PCD 36 at 45, 75, 105, 165, 195, 225, 285, 315, 345 deg, each with a Ø10 counterbore from the top to z 19 (5.6 below the face); the spigot is cut away between counterbores closer than 3 mm
- 3 x Ø6 H7 x 3.5 deep from the motor face, PCD 35.86 at 15, 135, 255 deg (the motor's own pins)
- Ø24 x 17 blind bore from the motor face (mass)
- Ø40 spigot, 3 tall (general tolerance: the dowels locate the link; the spigot is three lobes between the counterbores and a lead into the link's recess)
- Walls of 1.4 mm (1.0 mm to the thread's major diameter) between the counterbores and the face's tapped and dowel holes, 15 deg apart: intended, fine in 6061
