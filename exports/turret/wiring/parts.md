# The turret's boards, part by part (v0)

Generated by `cad/turret_boards.py` from its COMPONENTS list; the circuit is docs/turret-wiring.md section 3 and the placements are the board pictures beside this file. Resistors are 1/4 W 1 % metal film unless the value says 1 W; "X7R" capacitors are 50 V radial ceramics; "film" capacitors are the timing parts. The catalogue key is the parts list's (exports/turret/bench/bom.json).
