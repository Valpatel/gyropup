## The custom parts (SendCutSend, one cart)

Each DXF is the part seen from the face its countersinks are cut in (the face a screw head goes into): upload it face up, as drawn, so the countersinks land on that face. Tapped holes are drawn at their tap drill (M5: Ø4.2, M3: Ø2.5).

- `pan_clamp`: upload `exports/turret/pan_clamp.dxf` at https://sendcutsend.com/materials/6061-t6-aluminum/ as **6061-T6 aluminium, 0.125 in (3.175 mm)**, quantity 1, deburred, no finish. The instant price shows on upload.
- `cradle_plate_l`: upload `exports/turret/cradle_plate_l.dxf` at https://sendcutsend.com/materials/6061-t6-aluminum/ as **6061-T6 aluminium, 0.125 in (3.175 mm)**, quantity 1, deburred, no finish; **4 holes countersunk 90 deg to Ø6.4 on the face drawn (the face on the rail; DIN 7991 M3 flat heads)**. The instant price shows on upload.
- `cradle_plate_r`: upload `exports/turret/cradle_plate_r.dxf` at https://sendcutsend.com/materials/6061-t6-aluminum/ as **6061-T6 aluminium, 0.125 in (3.175 mm)**, quantity 1, deburred, no finish; **the Ø4.2 hole tapped M5 x 0.8; 2 holes countersunk 90 deg to Ø6.4 on the face drawn (the face on the rail)**. The instant price shows on upload.
