# Print schedule: the first leg, its rig and the bench fixtures (Bambu P1S)

One P1S with a hardened nozzle and extruder, textured PEI with glue for PA6-CF. **339.0 h of printing** (pa6-cf 213.0 h, 3.25 kg, petg 85.6 h, 2.14 kg, tpu95 40.4 h, 0.40 kg), about **17.3 days** at 20 h a day, three weeks with drying and one reprint. The output adapters are machined (D46) and not here. Generated by `tools/print_schedule.py` from `exports/leg/print-plan.json`, `exports/rig/print-plan.json` and `exports/rig/bench/print-plan.json`.

## Drying

- **pa6-cf**: dry 8-12 h at 80 C, feed from a dry box (not the AMS); dry the next spool during the print before.
- **tpu95**: dry 6 h at 65 C; print slow, direct from the spool.
- **petg**: dry 4-6 h at 65 C if the spool was open.

## Rules

- The coupons (A) decide every seat before the big PA6-CF parts: if a coupon's seat other than the nominal one fits best, the leg's CAD takes that offset before D-I print (the main session's files); evaluate them while B prints.
- Print the hip frame (I) only after the coupons pass and the P1S has printed PA6-CF flat at 150 x 150; the fallback for a frame that warps is a split frame (the main session).
- The motors' bench tests need B's dyno and C's spider; B3 needs C's sleeves and A's sleeve block.
- Weigh every leg part: the rig's ballast table (exports/rig/rig.json) assumes the CAD's masses.
