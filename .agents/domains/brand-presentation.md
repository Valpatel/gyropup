# Gyropup's visual character

The user wants a bright, friendly robot brand with the depth of the approved prototype warning. On 2026-10-29 UTC they liked the motto text but rejected its racing-banner styling as cheesy. Their positive reference is `web/prototype-notice.css`, not a generic interpretation of “cool.”

- Keep marketing copy upright and readable, with sentence case and breathing room. Small uppercase labels are appropriate; italic all-caps slogans, chevrons, diagonal stripes and individually yellow words are not the preferred direction.
- Use layered navy surfaces, restrained amber accents, fine inner highlights and diffuse shadows for depth. Preserve the warm cream page and cool blue background behind the yellow robot.
- The warning remains the strongest surface. Reserve its hazard stripe, warning triangle and red risk badge for actual safety content. Marketing cards should be lighter and quieter.
- Preserve the exact motto: `Strike first, strike fast, strike last`.
- Verify contrast in normal and interactive states, wrapping, phone controls, loading composition and actual screenshots, then separately judge the design against the user's stated aesthetic reference. Functional checks cannot establish that the user likes a visual style.
