# Source guards do not label an old downloadable print plan

On 2026-10-29, independent publication review of integration `a294429` found that the directly accessible `docs/turret-print.md` still described every part as sliced and the stand as needing no decision. The current generator and source guards correctly rejected unbound old exports, but this previously generated page had no local historical marker or link to current readiness before its instructions. Rendered 390 px and 1440 px checks confirmed the omission. The archived `exports/turret/print/print.json` contains 21 rows and no current source receipts; the reviewed small insert-fit package is a different artifact family.

The [review finding](https://github.com/Valpatel/gyropup/pull/63#discussion_r4414800056) required a prominent local status notice, current-readiness/provenance links and a clear distinction between the historical full kit and the separate geometry-only fit specimens. The same review found the readiness entry point still described corrected carrier evidence and acquired retainer CAD as pending. Those summaries were refreshed against the integrated reviews and current production revision. The actual Build consumer uses the separate 24-row `exports/turret/bench/print-plan.json`; it copied old sliced values into a seemingly current queue. Both archived JSON families now identify their historical status. Build carries that status beside the turret queue, workstream and mixed totals, removing the aggregate notice when the turret is excluded. The existing browser harness exercises that real selection path and preserves other workstreams, model actions and totals.

Cause: improving a generator does not regenerate every previously published page, and readers can arrive directly at an old document without visiting its newer review index. Corrective rule: inspect actual published entry paths and every consumer of archived print metadata. Label historical files and unsupported readiness statements where they appear; do not change their old numbers to resemble current results. Preserve original evidence and link the actual current package with its separate scope.

This correction changes presentation and status, not geometry, slicing, fit or physical qualification. A successful document/link/browser check cannot close the mechanical gates. Future generated packages already emit their own research, completeness and slicing status; source-bound regeneration and independent engineering review remain mandatory.
