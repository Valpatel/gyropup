# A backing-foot fillet needs local construction and finished-solid checks

On 2026-11-08 UTC, PR265's genuine baseline/candidate geometry run at `6c373e51` built the retained zero-radius baseline in 105.71 s. The source-default 3.5 mm candidate failed in 22.98 s at `fivebar.py::_backing_foot_blend`, `local.fillet(radius, selected)`, with `StdFail_NotDone: BRep_API: command not done`. The baseline's body and bed checks passed; no candidate BREP, comparison, manufacturer clearance or FEA result exists. The original outcome remains FAILED. The [sealed failure proof](../plans/hip-study/whole-foot-attempt1-failure.json) records the six partial products, raw digests, full source/hardware/runtime equality and owned-group cleanup. [Independent classification](https://github.com/Valpatel/gyropup/issues/8#issuecomment-6715906793) confirms this is a native construction failure, not setup or a clearance rejection.

The helper named its solid `local`, but actually passed the whole intermediate web/back union into OCP's fillet builder. That is a construction-scope defect; the exact kernel failure cause is not established. The correction intersects actual existing material with a box two radii beyond the backing block, keeps the same source-owned concave contact selection and 3.5 mm radius, checks no retained local material was removed, then fuses only the addition into the original union. It does not insert artificial supporting material or weaken the requested radius. Failed fillets retain their exact selected-edge context and the owned unfilleted local/back BReps, without replacing the primary exception if diagnostic capture fails.

Corrective rule: a local fillet operation must use a truly local source-built solid; validate both local containment and the finished candidate. Keep the existing finished-solid checks for one valid solid, all baseline material, additions bounded to both intended feet, actual mounting/pilot measurements, body/bed fit, and measured manufacturer-model clearance. Intermediate fillet diagnostics cannot qualify a final solid after later booleans.

Limits: local construction is a proposed repair, not evidence that the 3.5 mm blend builds or improves strength. A fresh reviewed native comparison must establish that. The retained SF 1.60 remains below required 2.00; no physical acceptance follows from CAD, and no FEA is authorized by this change.
