    Warning: unsafe research prototypes

    Robots are dangerous. Do not build or operate these machines without proper training. Unexpected movement, falling robots, pinch and crush points, projectiles, high-power motors and batteries can cause serious injury or death and damage property. Electrical and fire hazards are also present.

    Gyropup prototypes are unsafe and will not be offered for sale. They are not intended for general public use. Designs are provided for research purposes only. Research work requires appropriate engineering and safety training, a controlled environment, and validated safeguards.

    Adult supervision alone does not make these prototypes safe. Supervised child and family play is a future goal requiring proven, validated designs. The browser simulation does not establish hardware safety.
