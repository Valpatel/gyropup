# Turret assembly

The order to build the turret in, from cad/turret_assembly.py (which draws the pictures from the CAD: the new
parts of each step in colour, lifted along the way they go in; what is already fitted in grey). Tools: a
soldering iron with heat-set insert tips (M3, M4, M5), metric hex keys 1.5-4 mm, DIN 471 circlip pliers for a
50 mm ring, a crimper for Micro-Fit and DT contacts (IWISS IWD-1620 class), cyanoacrylate gel.

## 1. The pan base and its inserts

![step 1](../exports/turret/assembly/step-01.png)

Heat-set inserts first, with the base on its foot: 5 x M3 in the clamp ears, 3 x M3 in the carrier bosses (from below), 4 x M3 in the base-board bosses, 4 x M4 in the DT pad, 5 short M3 in the bumper tabs.

## 2. The pan servo, its pinion and the DT12 receptacle

![step 2](../exports/turret/assembly/step-02.png)

The STS3215 drops into its pocket, bus plugs down, on its four posts: 4 x M2 x 20 up through the foot. The pinion on its horn: 4 x M3 x 16 (heads sunk). The DT04-12PA on the pad: 4 x M4 x 10; its 12 pins and the W12P wedge with the harness to the base board.

## 3. The base board and the index magnet

![step 3](../exports/turret/assembly/step-03.png)

The base board (the 555, the 12 V pan regulator, the 10 A main fuse, the ring's stator plug) on its four bosses: 4 x M3 x 6. The 3/16 in magnet glued on the housing's top at pan 0 (the clamp ring has its hole).

## 4. The pan bearing and its clamp

![step 4](../exports/turret/assembly/step-04.png)

The 6810-2RS pressed into its seat onto the Ø62 shoulder; the aluminium clamp ring (SendCutSend) over it: 5 x M3 x 8.

## 5. The turntable, its spacer and the circlip

![step 5](../exports/turret/assembly/step-05.png)

Before it goes in: 4 x M4 inserts in its top, the hall switch glued in the pocket underneath (leads up its hole). Its journal down through the inner race, the gear in mesh with the pinion; from below, through the foot's Ø65 opening, the spacer and the DIN 471-50 circlip in its groove (circlip pliers).

## 6. The ring driver and the slip ring on its carrier

![step 6](../exports/turret/assembly/step-06.png)

The driver pressed into the top of the turntable's bore (three crush ribs and a drop of CA). The ring screwed to its carrier (3 x M3 x 8 with washers), a 25 mm length of the 5/16 in tube on its rotor stub, its rotor leads gathered; from below, the ring goes up the bore, the tube into the driver's socket and the leads out through the driver's crescent; the carrier's tabs into their pockets: 3 x M3 x 10 flat heads.

## 7. The tilt yoke and the turret-board bay

![step 7](../exports/turret/assembly/step-07.png)

The rotor leads through the yoke's slot, the yoke on the turntable: 4 x M4 x 10. 4 x M3 inserts in the -y arm, the bay on them: 4 x M3 x 8 flat heads; the three boards on their standoffs (12 and 14 mm); the ring's rotor plug and the hall into level 2.

## 8. The tilt servo and the cradle

![step 8](../exports/turret/assembly/step-08.png)

The STS3215 into its pocket on the +y arm, 4 x M2 x 10 from the arm's inner face. The MR126 into the -y arm's seat. The cradle: its hub onto the servo's horn (4 x M3 x 12), the ISO 7379 shoulder screw through the -y arm and the bearing into the cradle's M5 insert. Check the 0 and -25 deg stops by hand before anything else.

## 9. The laser

![step 9](../exports/turret/assembly/step-09.png)

The mount on the cradle's tab: 2 x M3 x 10 up through it; the laser in the mount on three nylon-tip M3 set screws (boresight them on the bench).

## 10. The cartridge: feed motor, dial, ESCs and wheels

![step 10](../exports/turret/assembly/step-10.png)

On the bench, not on the turret: the M2006 under the dial floor (3 x M3 x 6 flat), the dial on its D shaft (M3 x 8 and a washer from the top of the cone); the C610 on the base plate with two cable ties; the Snails up under the cage plate (4 x M3 x 6 each), each wheel on its bell (3 x M2 x 8 and two 1/16 in pins), the tyres stretched on (a drop of CA if they creep); the AM32 ESCs and the brake board on the plate.

## 11. The barrel, the hopper and the lid

![step 11](../exports/turret/assembly/step-11.png)

The barrel into its socket (a nylon-tip M3 set screw through the socket's insert), the gates' emitters and receivers in its bosses; the hopper on the housing (3 x M3 x 8); the lid; the flap on its 2 mm pin and spring.

## 12. The cartridge on the cradle, the bumper, the mount

![step 12](../exports/turret/assembly/step-12.png)

The cartridge down onto the cradle's two dowels, the knurled thumbscrew up into its insert (its O-ring keeps it in the rail); the two CT plugs (2 x 3 power, 2 x 6 signal). The bumper ring on its tabs: 5 x M3 x 20 and oversized washers. Then the turret on its stand or head ring: 2 x Ø5 dowels, 4 x M5 x 16, and the DT12 plug.
