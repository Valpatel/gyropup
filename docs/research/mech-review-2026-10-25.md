# Mechanical review, 2026-10-25 (rev 0.2 CAD, 22.2 kg physics model)

Independent review of the mechanical concept, the CAD (`cad/`), the physics model (`sim/`), the 3D pages
(`designer/`), the FEA (`cad/studies.py`) and the turret concept. Severity: **blocker** (the machine would not work
or the numbers it rests on are wrong), **major** (must change before parts are ordered), **minor** (fix when touched).
Numbers quoted as "measured" come from re-running the simulator today
(`scratchpad/torque_map.py`: it re-runs six scenarios and records the controller's joint torques every step).

Verdict in one line: the **concept is right** (coaxial hip + knee at the body, a parallelogram knee, hub-motor wheels,
the J501 box as the body, the lidar under the waist), but the **knee drive as drawn has its dead point in the middle of
the working range**, the **physics model does not represent that drive** (so motor loads and limits are wrong), the
made parts are **2x too heavy**, and the **head, battery and fall cases have no load path or test**. The plan is in
`docs/cad-plan.md`.

## A. Leg and knee drive

| # | Sev | Finding | Evidence | Fix |

| A1 | **blocker** | **The push-rod parallelogram has its change point (dead point) at a 90° knee, inside the working range.** The shin lever is drawn perpendicular to the shin and the crank perpendicular to the thigh at the straight pose, so the transmission angle is φ = γ − 90° (γ = interior knee angle). ## What is right and should stay

- Coaxial hip + knee at the body with a parallelogram (fix the lever, A1): light legs, both big motors on the body.
- The support bearing in the motor plate (RobStride's 45 × 58 × 7 output bearing has no moment rating).
- Aluminium for anything a motor or bearing bolts to; the FEA numbers that killed the printed yoke and thigh.
- Lidar under the waist; cameras front and rear; the VN-100 rigid on the body.
- The cable rules and the service-loop check (`cad/cables.py`), extended to the new lever geometry.
- The closed-loop toolchain itself (`trial_eval.py` → CAD → sim → FEA → cables) is the right skeleton for tonight's loop.
