## Checked on arrival

- **Rails:** both blocks run smooth with no play by hand; the rail's hole pitch (60) and end distance (20) as drawn.
- **Pack adapter:** a charged pack reads 54-60 V at its leads (the 60 V mode engaged).
- **Contactor:** the + terminal goes to the pack side (its contacts are polarised).
- **Weight plates:** weigh each; the ballast table in `exports/rig/rig.json` assumes 0.567 and 1.134 kg.
- **Motors:** firmware version (`python -m rig.rs04 --id 127 version`); the 310 mm leads and their XT30 and GH plugs.

## Considered, not bought

- **RobStride Discharge Module** (the regeneration bleeder, rcdrone $47.55, sold out 2026-10-27): the power-off damping B1 measures is the motor's own `damper` switch (parameter 0x702A), which needs no module; and the module starts bleeding at 64 V, above the RS-04's 60 V trip, so it would not protect the rig's bus. The pack absorbs regeneration; jumps start at or under 57.5 V - 27 A x the pack's resistance (docs/leg-bench-tests.md).
- **HGR20 rails** as the stronger backup: their 44 mm blocks overlap at the post's 40 mm slot spacing.
- **80/20 extrusions:** a different slot; only as a whole-frame swap.
- **A rocker switch on the pod:** the pack's XT60 is the master disconnect; the contactor only closes with the pendant held.
- **The load cells under jumps:** 3.4 kN would crush 50 kg cells; the load plate is for stand and squat only.
