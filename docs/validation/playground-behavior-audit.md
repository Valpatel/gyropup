## Experience judgment and limits

The bright curved guide and visible jar pour make the main battle loop easier to understand. The large ARM, RELOAD and FIRE controls communicate the central actions well. Inspection modes and the journey are useful demonstrations of the machine, while the portal exposes the project's progress and engineering evidence. Those are visual and interaction judgments, not proof that children will find the game fun.

A human play session should still assess aiming satisfaction, touch ergonomics while holding ARM and firing, pacing, audio balance, emotional appeal and whether the first minute explains itself. The automated checks do not establish hardware reliability, physical projectile behavior, collision safety, real microphone/voice quality, network-filter reputation, every possible phone/browser combination or accessibility conformance. Representative phone layouts are tested rather than claiming every phone resolution exists as a finite test set.
