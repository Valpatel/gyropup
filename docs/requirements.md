# Requirements

Each requirement in the user's own words, the feature that meets it, and how we check it. Statuses are updated as the work lands.

| # | The user said | Feature | Check | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | "design a robot for me like the diablo robot" | Self-balancing wheel-legged biped: two legs (hip + knee), a driven wheel at each foot | Mass, torque and balance budgets in the designer; simulation in MuJoCo, then Isaac Sim | open |
| R2 | "find the most available hub motors and actuators that are a bit chunky" | Catalog of buyable QDD leg actuators and hub motors, with seller, stock and date checked | `catalog/*.json` items carry `buy` links, `in_stock` and `price_checked` | open |
| R3 | "a choose your own adventure book web based guided design where I pick parts, you tell me pricing and such" | `designer/`: chapter-by-chapter guide; every choice shows price, mass and what it changes | Every option has a price and a source; the totals match the BOM | open |
| R4 | "make sure it can all be 3d printed but then swap out for parts made by sendcutsend metal brakcets, carbon fiber rods, whatever we need" | Every structural part has a printed version and, where useful, a SendCutSend / CF tube version with the same interfaces | Each part lists its fabrication options; sheet parts pass SendCutSend's design rules | open |
| R5 | "make a new private repo under valpatel with a cute name for a robot" | Private repo `Valpatel/wobbit` | `gh repo view` shows PRIVATE | open |
| R6 | "make the robot designer tool as important as the robot" | The designer is a first-class deliverable: tested model, catalog, docs | Model unit tests; the designer's output builds the CAD and the sim model | open |
| R7 | "Use our valpatel agent-cad and improve that agent-cad as we work" | Project runs on `cad-agent`; generic pieces (robot export, mass properties, BOM/cost, sheet-metal rules) go into cad-agent | cad-agent tests for each addition | open |
| R8 | "I want the robot simulated in Isaac-sim eventually for RL training" | URDF + meshes with CAD mass and inertia, actuator limits; USD via Isaac Sim's importer; Isaac Lab task later | URDF validity and inertia checks; MuJoCo load and balance test | open |
| R9 | "assume jetson orin agx 32GB with our new compute box" | The J501 compute box (206 × 181 × 112 mm, 12–36 V in) is the body payload | Mass and power budgets include it; the battery voltage suits it, or a DC-DC is in the BOM | open |
| R10 | "Ideally this is mostly 3d printed for low cost and we use a fancier bambu printer to make it a carbon fiber filled material later on. But start with PLA" / "(Bambu P1S to start)" | PLA on a P1S is the default for every printed part; a material ladder (PLA → PETG → CF-filled) rescales parts | Every part fits a 256 mm bed; stress and heat margins are computed for the chosen material | open |
| R11 | "Take notes from that new hugging face duck robot, cute and fun but works." | Character design (proportions, head/eyes, expressive motion) and the Open Duck Mini build and RL lessons | `research/reference-robots.md` has the lessons; the designer has a character chapter | open |
| R13 | "our main goal is to use the jetson orin agx because that's what we have, along with vlp-16 lidar, zed x or zed2i camera, intel realsense depth camera, just sticking with parts we have but list them as preferred compoennts" / "don't list owned parts as owned in the repo, just list their price and separate sensors/compute from chassis and actuators." | The AGX Orin J501 box, VLP-16, ZED X or ZED 2i and RealSense are the preferred defaults, listed at retail price. The BOM has separate **Sensors & compute** and **Chassis & actuators** sections with their own subtotals. A sensor head holds them | `catalog/sensors_compute.json` (`preferred: true`, no ownership field); mass and power budgets include the full sensor payload; the lidar has a clear 360° view | open |
| R14 | "Honestly the real goal should be closed loop - design a robot, evaluate it in sim, look at the stress points and how well it can drive around etc, iterate independently. Any components, any specs, choose your own adventure robot design + sim + manufacturing." | Closed loop: design (catalog or custom parts with any specs) → CAD + robot model → MuJoCo scenarios (balance, push, drive course, slope, curb, squat, hop) → sim loads → per-part FEA stress → manufacturing checks and cost → an optimizer or agent iterates without a human in the loop | `tools/evaluate.py design.json` writes one scored report; `tools/iterate.py` improves a design over many evaluations; the designer shows the sim and stress results | open |
| R12 | "Don't rush" | Research first, decisions with sources, verified outputs | Every number cites a source or is marked as an estimate | ongoing |
