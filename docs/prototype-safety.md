# Research prototype warning

**Warning: robots are dangerous. Do not build or operate them without proper training. High-power machines can cause serious injury or death and damage property.**

Gyropup prototypes are unsafe and will not be offered for sale. They are not for general public use. Designs are provided for research purposes only.

Our goal is future play with children and families using proven final designs and responsible adult supervision. Today's prototypes are not established as safe for that use. Adult supervision does not replace training, validation or a proven design.

## Public presentation

The website shows this warning before entry, with an explicit **I understand** acknowledgement. The warning remains available at the bottom of the landing and document pages. The acknowledgement records that the notice was displayed; it is not a legal waiver, proof of training or a safety certification. It never enables physical hardware.

Acknowledgement lasts for the current browser-tab session and is tied to the notice revision. Updating the notice revision requires a new acknowledgement. Opening the notice silences speech and sound without changing saved volume preferences or destroying the current scene or conversation.

The public design targets are **up to 100 lb (45.36 kg)** and **$10,000**. These are design targets for an evolving research project. The cost figure is not a current price or an offer to sell a prototype, and no validated cost basis is implied. Existing CAD masses, load reports and parts estimates retain their actual inputs.
