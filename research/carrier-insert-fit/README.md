# Carrier insert-fit specimens

Issue #89 / parent #66. Small, **unpowered fit specimens under development**, cut from the current carrier and pan-base CAD. They are intended to collect measured hole and insert seating data before a larger print. No physical trial has been performed.

The carrier specimen will preserve its exterior-face-down orientation, the Ø7 boss, Ø4 blind pilot and surrounding plate/edge. The base-anchor specimen will preserve foot-down printing and its horizontal Ø4 blind pilot. These are different layer orientations; one cannot qualify the other.

The selected insert is McMaster **94459A140**, M3×0.5. The cached manufacturer specification from 2026-10-26 states 5.7 mm installed length, maximum hole diameter4 mm and minimum material thickness6.5 mm. The normalized manufacturer CAD is5.74 mm long. These facts do not prescribe a proven PA6-CF heat-setting process or establish the strength of the current boss. Private manufacturer CAD is not published.

The first candidate will retain the nominal pilot rather than choose a diameter correction without measurements. Print recipes will be recorded from the carrier/base configuration, separately from any actual sliced or printed result. A three-specimen measurement sheet for each orientation will remain blank until real observations exist.

Remaining: generate source-bound STEP/STL geometry and recipes, validate the extracted local geometry and output meshes, publish inspection links, and obtain independent review. This package will not qualify complete-base strength, retention torque, service/tool access, electrical isolation, detailed thread mating, or powered operation.
