- **SF** is the isotropic gate value each record reports; **verdict** is what the turret record judges its printed parts on (its layer-aware SF), shown before -> after.
- **#197** is cad-agent #197's projection: the recorded SF times the new strength over the old.
- **stress** is the re-run's design stress over the record's. At 1.000 the part and its loads are unchanged, so only the material moved the SF, and the projection is exact. Away from 1 the part or its loads changed after the record was made.
- **v0.4.2 today** is today's part under the old data: the difference from **SF before** is the design's change, from **SF after** the material's. For the leg it is a run of its own (`evidence/leg-v042-today.json`: `cad/leg_studies.py` at the same revision with cad-agent v0.4.2). For the turret it is the old strength over today's design stress, which is exact for a part of one material under force loads (`evidence/historical-scaling-control.json`, and the leg's two runs).
- **deflection** is the re-run's largest displacement over the record's, with the modulus ratio in brackets.
